How it works

Four steps from lost to returned

A simple, peer-to-peer lost and found system for university students and faculty.

STEP 01

Search the listings

Before posting, check the Browse page. Someone may have already found your item and listed it with a campus location.

STEP 02

Share the details

If not listed, post a concise report specifying location, date, and distinguishing features known only to the real owner.

STEP 03

Connect directly

Reach out securely using university email. No third-party accounts or phone numbers required.

STEP 04

Hand it over safely

Verify unique proof of ownership, then arrange a meeting in daylight at a busy campus location.
