A good report gets the item home

  1. Say what makes it yoursColour, brand, stickers, scratches, what's inside.
  2. Be exact about the placePick the closest location and mention the room or bench.
  3. Add a photo if you have oneListings with a picture are easier to recognise.

Never put PINs, passwords, card numbers or ID numbers in a public description. Keep one detail back so the real owner can prove it's theirs.
