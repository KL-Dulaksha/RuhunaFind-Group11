    How it works

    Three steps from lost to returned.

    The full walkthrough

    1. Step 01

      Search the listings

      Look through recent campus reports. Someone may have already found your item and listed it.

    2. Step 02

      Post a report

      Can't find it? Post a short report with a description, the date and the place it was lost or found.

    3. Step 03

      Reconnect safely

      Get in touch by university email and arrange a handover somewhere busy on campus.

    Found something? Post it before someone starts searching.

    It takes about a minute, and you don't need an account.
